Who we are

Simple Wars (the “Game”) is a mobile strategy game developed by Yehor Ivanov using Unity. It is published as Grib Games on Google Play for Android and Yehor Ivanov on the App Store for iOS. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean the developer operating the Game.

We are responsible for the personal-data processing whose purposes and means we determine. Google and Apple also process information through their own services, under their respective privacy policies.

This policy covers Simple Wars, its advertising and in-app purchases, support correspondence, and these legal webpages. Privacy contact: yigorka812@gmail.com.

Basic play does not require a separate account created with us. Reading this policy or accepting the User Agreement does not itself give consent to optional advertising personalization or tracking.

Google AdMob advertising

Simple Wars uses Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display advertising in advertising-enabled versions. Ad formats and availability depend on the installed version. Where rewarded ads are offered, the Game uses the completion result to grant the benefit shown before the ad.

Google processes advertising data to deliver and measure ads, operate its services, and help detect invalid traffic. Depending on the device, SDK configuration, and permissions, identifiers can include Android's advertising ID or Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). Other app- or device-scoped identifiers may be used for permitted operational purposes. Access to an identifier does not mean that all forms of tracking are permitted.

Google's processing is explained in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. These sources also explain Google's controls and processing outside the Game.

Ads are provided by third parties. Opening an advertisement or advertiser's website brings you under that service's own terms and privacy practices.

Advertising choices and tracking permissions

Where required, we obtain your consent before optional advertising personalization or access to device information. Use the choices in the consent message shown in the Game. Where an advertising privacy-options entry point is required, you can reopen it to review or change those choices. You can also contact us with questions about the choices available in your version.

On iOS, tracking across other companies' apps and websites requires Apple's permission where applicable. You can manage that permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Denying tracking does not remove access to basic gameplay. Apple explains these controls in its App Tracking Transparency guidance.

On Android, available advertising-ID controls are in the device's privacy or ads settings; their names differ by device and Android version. Advertising consent, operating-system tracking permissions, and a decision to watch a rewarded ad are separate choices.

Non-personalized advertising is not necessarily data-free: limited information may still be processed to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and report performance, subject to applicable requirements. Changing a setting does not automatically erase information already held by a provider.

In-app purchases

Optional digital purchases are processed by Google Play on Android or Apple's App Store on iOS. The store handles payment credentials. We do not receive your full payment-card number or your store-account password.

The Game receives purchase information needed to verify a transaction, recognize an entitlement, and restore eligible content. If you contact support, we may ask for an order reference to investigate. The store separately keeps payment and account records under its own policies.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. Purchase conditions and refund information appear in the User Agreement.

We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or security, or in a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards and required notice.

Google, Apple, and other service providers may process information outside the country where you live. Provider policies explain the arrangements for their services; you may contact us about processing we control.

Advertising-related disclosures can count as “sale”, “sharing”, or targeted advertising under certain laws, even without a direct payment for a particular player's data. Where those laws apply, you may exercise the relevant opt-out rights through the available advertising privacy controls or by contacting us.

Legal bases where applicable

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on performance of our agreement to provide requested game and purchase features, consent for optional processing that requires it, legitimate interests in responding to support requests and protecting service security where those interests are not overridden by your rights, and compliance with legal obligations.

You may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal. We do not treat acceptance of the User Agreement as consent to advertising tracking.

Retention and deletion

  • Device data: saved games and preferences remain until removed, overwritten by the Game's save-management behavior, or deleted through device/app-storage controls. Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local progress. Device or platform backups may retain separate copies.
  • Advertising data: Google keeps information under its own retention policies and applicable requirements. We do not control every provider-held record or promise a single retention period for all advertising data.
  • Purchase records: entitlement and transaction records are retained as needed to provide paid benefits, handle refunds or disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. The stores retain their own records.
  • Support records: we retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to address the request and any related legal obligation or dispute, then delete or anonymize it when no longer necessary.

To request deletion of personal data we control, email yigorka812@gmail.com and mention Simple Wars. We may need proportionate identifying information to locate records. If records must be retained for a legal reason, we will explain the relevant limitation. Uninstalling the Game does not automatically delete data already processed by Google, Apple, or an email provider.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to certain processing, withdraw consent, and opt out of qualifying targeted advertising, sale, or sharing. You may also complain to the competent data-protection authority. Where provided by law, you can appeal a refusal by replying to our response.

Send requests to yigorka812@gmail.com. We respond within applicable legal time limits and may verify your request using information proportionate to the risk. We do not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right. For information held independently by Apple or Google, their own privacy tools may also be needed.

Children and younger players

A store content rating is not, by itself, permission to process a child's personal data. Where we know a player is below an applicable age for independent privacy consent, we do not carry out processing that requires a parent or guardian's consent without that consent. Advertising for known child or under-age users is subject to the restrictions required by the platform and applicable law.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal data has been collected from a child without the safeguards required by law, contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion of information we control where required.
