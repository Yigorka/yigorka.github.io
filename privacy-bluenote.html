Who we are

BlueNote is a notes and to-do application developed by Yehor Ivanov. In this policy, “we”, “us”, and “our” mean the developer of BlueNote. Contact us at yigorka812@gmail.com.

This policy covers the BlueNote app, its widgets, and support correspondence. It describes the data used to provide the app's features and the choices available to you. BlueNote does not require a separate BlueNote account. Apple services, such as iCloud and the App Store, use your Apple Account and are also subject to Apple's own terms and privacy information.

The developer does not operate a server that stores a copy of your notes. Notes sent by you in a support message or exported to a destination you choose are separate from the app's normal local and iCloud storage.

iCloud synchronization

When iCloud is available and enabled for BlueNote, the app synchronizes lists and tasks through a private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. This allows compatible devices using that iCloud account to access the same information.

Apple operates iCloud and processes the account, service, and technical information needed to provide it. You can manage iCloud access through your device's Apple Account settings. Sync depends on your connection, account settings, available iCloud storage, and Apple's service availability.

Deleting a note or list in the app is intended to synchronize that deletion to your other connected devices. Synchronization can be delayed while a device is offline or a service error is unresolved. Uninstalling BlueNote does not necessarily remove its iCloud data or any device backups. Use the available iCloud storage controls to manage cloud copies.

Microphone and speech recognition

Dictation is optional. BlueNote asks for microphone and speech-recognition permissions before using this feature. The app requests on-device recognition when the selected language and device support it. Where on-device recognition is unavailable, Apple's recognition service may transmit and process audio on Apple's servers.

BlueNote does not save audio recordings as note attachments or send dictation audio to a developer-operated server. The resulting transcription is inserted into the text you are editing and, if saved, is handled like your other note content. Apple's handling of audio and service information is governed by Apple's applicable privacy information.

You can stop dictation and manage microphone or speech-recognition permission in system settings. Denying these permissions does not prevent you from typing notes.

Face ID and app locking

On supported devices, you can choose to protect access to BlueNote with Face ID. Authentication is performed by the operating system. BlueNote receives an authentication result and does not receive or store your facial image, biometric template, or device passcode.

The app keeps the preference indicating whether app locking is enabled. Where permitted by the app and operating system, device authentication may offer a passcode fallback. App locking controls access to the app's interface; it does not replace the separate security and access settings for iCloud, exported files, or your email.

Reminders, widgets, and exports

Reminders: notifications are scheduled locally when you request them and permit notifications. Their content may be visible on your Lock Screen or in notification history, depending on your system settings. Manage notification access and preview visibility in those settings.

Widgets: BlueNote's widget extension uses a local shared snapshot to show tasks and apply completion actions. When BlueNote's Face ID lock is enabled, the app is designed to hide note content in its widgets and prevent widget completion. Removing a widget does not delete the underlying notes.

Exports and backups: the app can create backup or text files when you request an export. Those files can contain readable note content. Their destination, sharing, and subsequent retention are under your control; deleting a note in BlueNote does not delete copies you previously exported.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes payment for BlueNote Pro. The app uses verified StoreKit transaction information to determine whether Pro is active, restore purchases, and respond to expiry or revocation. BlueNote does not ask for your Apple Account password and does not receive payment card or bank account details.

Subscription management, payment records, and refund requests are handled through Apple. Apple's services are described in its Privacy Policy and relevant service-specific privacy notices. We do not have direct control over Apple's account records or retention.

Advertising, analytics, and disclosure

BlueNote does not display advertising, use advertising identifiers, or track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Its current app features do not include a third-party behavioral analytics SDK. We do not sell your notes or use their contents for targeted advertising.

Data is handled by Apple as needed for the platform features described above, and by our email provider when you contact support. If you share an export or follow an external link, the recipient's or service provider's own practices also apply.

For information actually held by the developer, such as a support message, we may disclose relevant information where legally required or necessary to address a security incident or legal claim, subject to applicable law. We do not have a developer-hosted archive of your private note database to disclose.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing needed to provide the app features or purchase access you request is based on performing our agreement with you. Optional processing uses your consent where legally required. Support and limited security or troubleshooting activity may rely on legitimate interests, balanced against your rights, or on a legal obligation where one applies.

System permission prompts control access to device capabilities. Reading this policy or using a paid plan is not consent to unrelated tracking. We do not introduce a tracking feature through this policy.

Apple and email providers may process information outside your country under their own service arrangements. Where the developer is responsible for a transfer, we use the safeguards required by the law applicable to that transfer. Contact us about processing relevant to your request.

Retention and deletion

  • App data: notes and preferences remain in app storage until you remove them or the app's data is removed. Retained backups can contain earlier copies.
  • iCloud data: synced data remains subject to your app deletions, iCloud settings, and Apple's retention practices. Removing the app alone does not necessarily delete cloud copies.
  • Dictation: BlueNote uses live audio buffers for recognition and does not create an archive of audio recordings. Saved transcripts are retained as note text. Apple's processing follows its own applicable service information.
  • Widgets: the app updates or replaces its local widget snapshot as relevant content and access settings change. The operating system may retain displayed widget or notification content temporarily.
  • Purchases: current entitlement information is used for access checks. Apple retains transaction and billing records under its own policies and applicable obligations.
  • Support: we keep correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up or disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. We delete it when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

To request deletion of information held by the developer, email yigorka812@gmail.com. We can assist with app storage questions, but cannot directly delete your Apple Account, Apple's purchase history, or files held by recipients of your exports.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or receive a portable copy of your personal data; object to certain processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Contact yigorka812@gmail.com and identify BlueNote. We may request proportionate information to verify your request and locate records we hold. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or unrelated private notes.

Children and younger users

BlueNote is a general-purpose productivity app and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent us information that should not have been collected can contact us to request investigation and appropriate deletion.

Parents and guardians can use Apple's device, permission, and purchase controls where appropriate. The store age rating does not replace any parental consent required by applicable law.

Security

BlueNote uses operating-system storage and authentication facilities for its local features and Apple's infrastructure for iCloud and StoreKit. Access to your device, Apple Account, exported files, and email should be protected using the controls available for those services.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to information held by us, including support correspondence, to protect it from unauthorized access or misuse. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.
