What BlueNote provides

BlueNote helps you create notes and task lists, organize entries, add descriptions and priorities, use dictation, and schedule reminders. Compatible versions can synchronize lists through iCloud and offer Face ID locking on supported devices.

BlueNote Pro provides additional features such as recurring tasks, subtasks, and interactive Home Screen widgets. Availability depends on the installed version, device capabilities, permissions, and any plan shown in the app. The purchase screen describes the offer available to you before you confirm payment.

No separate BlueNote login is required. Apple Account access may be needed for iCloud and App Store purchases.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

You can manage or cancel an Apple-billed subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select BlueNote. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

Deleting BlueNote or removing a widget does not cancel your subscription. Cancel before the renewal or trial end date shown by Apple. Cancellation takes effect according to Apple's purchase terms and applicable law; access generally remains until the end of the current entitlement period.

Subscription expiry does not by itself delete your notes. Creating or configuring Pro features may require an active entitlement.

Restoring purchases and refunds

On a compatible device or after reinstalling the app, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. BlueNote checks available verified entitlements and provides a Restore Purchases action. Monthly and annual Pro require an unexpired entitlement; a lifetime purchase does not require subscription renewal. Account settings and Apple's service availability can affect restoration.

Apple manages App Store billing and refund requests. You can request an eligible refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or follow Apple's refund instructions. Eligibility is determined under Apple's terms and applicable law. These terms do not exclude any mandatory refund or consumer rights.

A refunded, revoked, or expired transaction may no longer provide Pro access.

iCloud, devices, and backups

Synchronization uses Apple's iCloud service and a private database associated with your iCloud account. Devices must have compatible settings, account access, storage, and connectivity. Your iCloud account and the Apple Account used for purchases serve different functions; restoring Pro does not itself restore notes.

Changes made offline may take time to reach other devices. Sync is not an independent historical backup of every earlier version of a note. Deletions can synchronize to other devices, so use the export or backup features when you want to keep a separate copy.

Removing the app can remove local app data while cloud copies or device backups may remain. Keep any needed backup before uninstalling or changing storage settings. We cannot recover notes that are no longer present in your device storage, iCloud, or a backup available to you.

Reminders, dictation, widgets, and Face ID

Reminders and repeated tasks: these features depend on saved dates, calendar and time-zone settings, notification permissions, and the operating system. Delivery or widget refresh can be delayed. Verify important dates and use appropriate alternative arrangements where timely delivery is essential.

Dictation: speech recognition can make mistakes and may require an internet connection when on-device recognition is unavailable. Review the transcription before relying on it.

Widgets: widgets show a snapshot and may refresh after the app's data changes. Device restrictions and background activity can affect updates. When BlueNote's Face ID lock is enabled, note content and completion actions are restricted in the widgets.

Face ID: app locking relies on the operating system and supported hardware. It controls access to the app interface and does not secure copies you export or choose to share elsewhere.
