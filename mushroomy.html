Mushroomy

What the app does

Seasonal calendar

Browse mushroom activity through the year and use the calendar as a planning aid for your own foraging trips.

Species reference

Explore a growing reference collection with names, seasons, basic characteristics, and educational illustrations.

Foraging conditions

Use weather-based indicators and reminders to identify potentially interesting days to check local habitats.

Photo calendar

Keep a personal visual record of your mushroom finds and memorable days directly on your device.

⚠️ Important safety notice

Mushroomy is an educational and planning companion — not a mushroom identification, medical, food-safety, or edibility decision tool. Never eat, taste, handle, or prepare a mushroom based only on information, forecasts, names, descriptions, photos, icons, or illustrations shown in the app. Mushroom appearance varies widely, dangerous species can resemble edible species, and the app's illustrations may be simplified, stylized, incomplete, or inaccurate.

Always verify any mushroom using multiple authoritative sources and, when edibility matters, consult a qualified local mycologist or other competent expert. The decision to collect, handle, prepare, or consume any mushroom is solely the user's responsibility.
